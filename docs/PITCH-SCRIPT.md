# margin — English pitch

Approximate delivery: 2–3 minutes. Press N in the HTML presentation for slide-specific speaker notes.

We already use AI to study every day. It can be incredibly helpful. But the moment I get stuck on one step of an exercise, I have to leave the place where I am actually thinking. That is the problem we wanted to solve.

In my own experience, asking for help means taking a screenshot, sharing it, opening a chat, and rebuilding the context. Sometimes I even move it through WhatsApp to get it onto my computer. By the time I ask the question, I have lost my concentration.

Then I have to explain everything again: here is the course material, here is what I have tried, here is where I got stuck. And please, do not give me the whole solution. Because getting the answer is not the same as learning how to reach it.

That is why we built margin: a notebook with an AI tutor right beside your work. Write naturally, select the step that is holding you back, and ask for help. The tutor reads the current page and connects its guidance to that exact point. You stay in your notebook.

The tutor offers a question or a useful next step. You try again, then ask it to review your update. Learning notes keep a record of the help you received and observations from your attempts. This gives later guidance context, while keeping the reasoning in your hands.

You can also attach your course notes. margin can use relevant text excerpts to inform its guidance and link a supporting quotation to the document and page. Your materials stay associated with the notebook, so you do not have to start from scratch with every question.

And when words are not enough, margin can draw directly on the page: arrows, force diagrams, labels and short examples. The elements appear progressively. OpenAI voice explains the sketch while the relevant detail is highlighted. You can see the connection as you hear it. Let us show you.

Our goal is simple: help students keep their concentration and take the next step themselves. The next step should happen on your page. margin. Write. Think. Understand.
